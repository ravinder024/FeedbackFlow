# Go-To-Market Strategy

## 1. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
- **Target Audience**: 
    - B2B SaaS companies with 10-500 employees.
    - Product teams conducting user testing.
    - Companies frustrated with screenshot-based feedback tools.
    - Privacy-conscious organizations requiring GDPR compliance.

## 2. Market Positioning
- **Tagline**: "Figma for Feedback" — collaborative, visual, real-time.
- **Differentiators**:
    - Privacy-first alternative to Hotjar/FullStory.
    - Structured workflows for feedback collection vs. ad-hoc tools.
    - Seamless integration with existing product development pipelines.

## 3. First 100 Users Strategy
- **Beta Program**:
    - Handpick testers from target ICP.
    - Offer exclusive early access and direct support.
- **Outreach Channels**:
    - Product Hunt and Indie Hackers for visibility.
    - LinkedIn for professional networking.
    - Personalized outreach to product managers and UX teams.
- **Iteration**:
    - Collect feedback from beta users to refine the product.
    - Develop case studies showcasing early adopter success stories.

## 4. Pricing Strategy (MVP)
- **Free Tier**:
    - 1 test group, 3 test members, 50 pins/month.
- **Starter Plan**: $29/month
    - 5 test groups, 10 members, unlimited pins.
- **Team Plan**: $99/month
    - Unlimited groups, 50 members, advanced analytics.
- **Enterprise Plan**: Custom pricing
    - Includes SSO, on-premise hosting, SLA agreements.

## 5. Launch Channels
- **Product Hunt**: 
    - Launch campaign to attract early adopters.
- **LinkedIn**:
    - Publish thought leadership content targeting product teams.
- **Content Marketing**:
    - Blog posts on user testing best practices and feedback workflows.
- **Developer Communities**:
    - Engage with Dev.to and Hashnode to reach technical audiences.

## 6. Success Metrics
- **Key Goals**:
    - 100 signups in the first month.
    - 20% activation rate (users creating their first test group).
    - 10 paying customers within the first quarter.

## Competitive Differentiation
- **Privacy-First Approach**:
    - GDPR compliance and anonymized data handling.
- **Collaborative Features**:
    - Real-time feedback visualization and team workflows.
- **Ease of Use**:
    - Intuitive interface designed for non-technical users.
- **Scalability**:
    - Flexible plans catering to startups and enterprises alike.
